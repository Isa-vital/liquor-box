Our Story

A cellar built for Kampala's evenings.

Liquor Box started with a simple frustration: finding a good bottle in Kampala after dark meant either settling for what the nearest kiosk had, or crossing town in traffic and hoping the shop was still open.

So we built the shop we wished existed — a curated cellar of whiskey, wine, beer and spirits, checked for provenance, priced honestly, and delivered to your door in under an hour across Kampala and Wakiso.

Order on the site or send us a message on WhatsApp. Pay by Mobile Money or cash. Our rider checks ID at the door, every time — no exceptions.

What We Stand By

Three promises

01
Genuine bottles only

Every bottle is sourced from licensed distributors and checked for provenance before it reaches the shelf.

02
Honest prices

What you see is what you pay. Delivery fees are quoted upfront by zone — no surprises at the door.

03
Responsible always

We sell to verified adults 18+ only. Our riders check ID on every delivery, and we never deliver to anyone visibly intoxicated.
