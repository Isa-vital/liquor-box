Policy

Age verification policy

1. We sell to adults only

Liquor Box Store sells alcoholic beverages exclusively to persons aged 18 years and above, in line with the laws of Uganda. By placing an order, you confirm that you are of legal drinking age.

2. ID check on every delivery

Our rider will ask the recipient to present a valid government-issued photo ID (National ID, passport or driving permit) on arrival. If the recipient cannot prove they are 18 or older, the delivery will not be completed. No ID, no delivery — no exceptions.

3. Refused deliveries

If a delivery is refused because of a failed age check, the order is cancelled. A delivery fee may still apply to cover the rider's trip. Repeated attempts to order under age may result in a permanent block.

4. No delivery to intoxicated persons

Our riders reserve the right to decline handover to any person who appears heavily intoxicated, in the interest of their safety and the safety of others.

5. Responsible enjoyment

We encourage all our customers to drink responsibly. Never drink and drive. If you or someone you know is struggling with alcohol use, please seek support from a qualified health professional.
